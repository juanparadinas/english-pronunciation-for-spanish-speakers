# English Pronunciation for Spanish Speakers

> Learn the English sounds, spelling rules, and stress that Spanish speakers
> are never taught — one page per sound, with the hidden rules made explicit.

**Reference accent:** SSBE (Southern Standard British English), with the main
differences to General American noted throughout.

**Read it online:**
https://juanparadinas.github.io/english-pronunciation-for-spanish-speakers/

## Who this is for

Native Spanish speakers who want to improve English pronunciation
systematically: the phonemes that do not exist in Spanish, how English spelling
maps to sound, and how stress and rhythm really work. Useful for self-study
and for teachers.

## What's inside

- **Foundations** — the phoneme inventory and the priority sounds to fix first.
- **Vowels and diphthongs** — one page per sound, in a two-angle format
  (letter → sound, and sound → spelling).
- **Consonants not in Spanish** — r, dark l, v, z, sh, th, and more.
- **Reading rules** — spelling-to-sound, the unwritten rules, silent letters,
  the letters A/U/OU, double consonants, and how R and W bend the vowel rules.
- **Stress and intonation** — word stress, noun/verb pairs, rhythm, intonation.
- **Reference** — minimal pairs, homophones, British vs American, study links.

## How it's organised

Every sound has its own page following the same structure: what it is and the
Spanish contrast, how to produce it, letter → sound, sound → spelling, minimal
pairs, the common Spanish error, and practice links. Start at the
[home page](https://juanparadinas.github.io/english-pronunciation-for-spanish-speakers/)
and follow the sidebar.

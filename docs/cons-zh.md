# Consonant: /ʒ/

> The voiced sibling of /ʃ/. Rare in English, and does not exist in standard
> Spanish. Mostly appears in -sion, -sure and French loans.

## 1. What it is

/ʒ/ is a voiced postalveolar fricative, the "s" in "pleasure", "vision". The
voiced partner of /ʃ/.

Spanish contrast: not in standard Spanish (some regional variants have it). It
is also rare in English, so it gets little practice.

## 2. How to produce it

Exactly like /ʃ/ but with voice: tongue behind the ridge, lips rounded, vocal
cords buzzing.

## 3. Angle 1 — spellings that make /ʒ/

| Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|
| si (in -sion after vowel) | vision, decision, confusion, television |
| su (in -sure) | pleasure, measure, leisure, treasure |
| s (in -sual/-sia) | usual, casual, Asia |
| g (French loans) | genre, beige, mirage, garage (UK) |
| z (rare) | azure, seizure |

## 4. Angle 2 — where the /ʒ/ sound is written (word lists)

pleasure, exposure, vision, genre, measure, closure, leisure, equation,
massage, azure, presume (variant), usual, casual, decision, confusion,
television, treasure, beige, mirage.

## 5. Minimal pairs / contrasts

/ʒ/ rarely forms clean minimal pairs; the key contrasts are:

| Pair | Contrast |
|---|---|
| pleasure / pressure | /ʒ/ vs /ʃ/ |
| version / virgin | /ʒ/ vs /dʒ/ |
| leisure / ledger | /ʒ/ vs /dʒ/ |

## 6. Common Spanish error

> The trap: replacing /ʒ/ with /dʒ/ (a hard j) or /ʃ/, so "vision" gets a
> "ch/j" sound.
> The fix: it is a smooth buzzing fricative, not a stop. Same as /ʃ/ with
> voice. Contrast pleasure/pressure.

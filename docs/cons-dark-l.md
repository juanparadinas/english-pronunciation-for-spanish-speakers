# Consonant: dark L /ɫ/

> English has two L sounds. The "dark" L, at the end of syllables, does not
> exist in Spanish and distorts the vowels around it.

## 1. What it is

English L has two variants:
- Light (clear) L: before a vowel, as in "light", "love". Close to Spanish L.
- Dark L /ɫ/: after a vowel or at syllable end, as in "table", "full", "milk".

Spanish contrast: Spanish has only the light L. Spanish speakers use a light L
everywhere, so "milk" and "table" sound wrong. The dark L also colours the
preceding vowel.

## 2. How to produce it

Dark L: keep the tongue tip down or lightly touching, and pull the BACK of the
tongue up and back toward the soft palate. It sounds almost like a "w" or "o"
colouring. In extreme cases it vocalises fully (L-vocalization): "milk" ->
"miuk".

## 3. Angle 1 — when L is dark

| Position | L type | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| before a vowel | light | light, love, lady, believe |
| after a vowel / syllable-final | dark | full, milk, table, cold, help |
| before a consonant | dark | milk, help, salt, old |
| syllabic (C + le) | dark | table, castle, purple, little |

## 4. Angle 2 — where dark L appears (word lists)

table, travel, trouble, pencil, level, equal, model, nasal, annual, moral,
royal, label, cancel, unveil, capitol, methanol, alcohol, control, lawful,
full, milk, cold, help, salt, old, bottle, little, apple, people.

## 5. Minimal pairs / contrasts

| Pair | Contrast |
|---|---|
| light / right | /l/ vs /r/ |
| full (dark l) vs foo | presence of dark l |
| feel / fear | dark l vs /ə/ (British) |
| milk (dark) vs mick | dark l colouring |

## 6. Common Spanish error

> The trap: using a light Spanish L at the end of words (table, full, milk).
> The fix: pull the back of the tongue up and back for syllable-final L. Let
> it colour the vowel. Practise "level up" and words ending in -le.

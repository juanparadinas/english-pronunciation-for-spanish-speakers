# Consonant: /θ/ (voiceless TH)

> The voiceless "th" in "think". In most of Spain it matches the "z/c" sound
> (zapato), but Latin American speakers replace it with /s/ or /t/.

## 1. What it is

/θ/ is a voiceless dental fricative, the "th" in "think", "bath", "month".

Spanish contrast: European Spanish has this sound (the "z" and soft "c" in
"zapato", "cielo"), so peninsular speakers produce it easily. Latin American
Spanish uses /s/ (seseo), so those speakers replace /θ/ with /s/ or /t/.

## 2. How to produce it

Tongue tip lightly between or against the top teeth, push voiceless air with
friction. No voice. It is the voiceless partner of /ð/.

## 3. Angle 1 — spellings that make /θ/

Always written th. /θ/ (voiceless) appears in:

| Position | Examples |
|---|---|
| content words (initial) | think, thing, three, thunder, Thursday, theatre |
| medial / final | bath, month, both, tooth, north, healthy, author |
| -th endings | mouth (noun), path, cloth, breath, fourth |

Contrast with /ð/: roughly, function words and -the verbs are voiced /ð/;
content words and most -th endings are voiceless /θ/.

## 4. Angle 2 — where the /θ/ sound is written

think, thing, three, thunder, thursday, theatre, theory, thick, thin, thread,
bath, month, both, tooth, north, south, healthy, author, nothing, something,
path, cloth, breath, fourth, mouth (noun), math, truth, death.

## 5. Minimal pairs / contrasts

| Pair | Contrast |
|---|---|
| think / sink | /θ/ vs /s/ |
| thing / sing | /θ/ vs /s/ |
| thin / tin | /θ/ vs /t/ |
| three / tree | /θ/ vs /t/ |
| bath / bat | /θ/ vs /t/ |
| mouth / mouse | /θ/ vs /s/ |

## 6. Common Spanish error

> The trap: Latin American speakers use /s/ (think = sink) or /t/ (three =
> tree).
> The fix: tongue to the teeth, voiceless friction. Peninsular speakers can
> reuse their "z/c" sound directly.

## /θ/ - /ð/ alternation (noun vs verb)

Spelling marks it: nouns end -th (voiceless /θ/), verbs end -the (voiced /ð/):
bath/bathe, breath/breathe, sheath/sheathe, teeth/teethe, wreath/wreathe. Also
north/northern, south/southern, worth/worthy. See
[phoneme-spelling reference](phoneme-spelling-reference.md).

Position rules (from the source):
- Initial th: /ð/ in function words (the, this, then, there); /θ/ in content
  words (think, through, throw). Exception: through /θ/.
- Medial th + -er/-ing: usually /ð/ (other, brother, bathing). Exceptions:
  panther, ether, anything/nothing.
- Final th: usually /θ/. Exceptions: with, smooth /ð/; booth, bequeath either.

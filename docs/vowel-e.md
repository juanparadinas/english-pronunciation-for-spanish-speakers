# Vowel: /e/ (short e)

> A short, front mid vowel. Close to Spanish "e", so relatively easy; the work
> is the contrasts with /æ/ and /ɪ/.

## 1. What it is

/e/ (also written /ɛ/) is a short, front, mid vowel, the sound in "bed", "red",
"head". Close to the Spanish "e".

Spanish contrast: very close to Spanish /e/, so one of the easiest. The risk is
confusing it with /æ/ (bed/bad) and, in weak syllables, with /ɪ/.

## 2. How to produce it

Tongue front and mid, lips slightly spread, short. Essentially the Spanish "e",
kept short.

## 3. Angle 1 — when a spelling makes /e/

| Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|
| e (closed syllable) | bed, red, pen, ten, when, left, send |
| ea | head, bread, dead, health, weather, ready |
| a (rare) | many, any, said, again |
| ai (rare) | said, again |

## 4. Angle 2 — where the /e/ sound is written

e (closed): bed, red, pen, ten, when, left, send, best, help, egg.
ea: head, bread, dead, health, weather, ready, breakfast, measure (stem).
a: many, any.
ai: said, again, against.
ie (rare): friend.
eo (rare): leopard, jeopardy.

## 5. Minimal pairs / contrasts

| Pair | Contrast |
|---|---|
| bed / bad | /e/ vs /æ/ |
| bet / bat | /e/ vs /æ/ |
| said / sad | /e/ vs /æ/ |
| ten / tin | /e/ vs /ɪ/ |
| bed / bid | /e/ vs /ɪ/ |
| pen / pin | /e/ vs /ɪ/ |

## 6. Common Spanish error

> The trap: merging /e/ with /æ/, so bed = bad.
> The fix: /e/ is the Spanish "e"; /æ/ is more open and leans toward "a".
> Train bed/bad and pen/pin to lock the boundaries.

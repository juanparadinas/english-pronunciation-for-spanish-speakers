# Minimal pairs

> Pairs of words that differ in exactly one phoneme. They are where Spanish
> speakers lose intelligibility, and the best drill for fixing a specific
> contrast. Grouped by the contrast being trained.

## Vowel contrasts

### /ɪ/ vs /iː/ (short vs long i)
ship/sheep, bit/beat, live/leave, sit/seat, fit/feet, rich/reach, it/eat,
fill/feel.

### /æ/ vs /ʌ/ (front a vs hat a)
cat/cut, cap/cup, ran/run, bat/but, match/much.

### /æ/ vs /e/
bad/bed, bat/bet, sad/said, man/men, had/head.

### /e/ vs /ɪ/
bed/bid, pen/pin, ten/tin, desk/disk.

### /ʌ/ vs /ɒ/
luck/lock, cut/cot, nut/not, duck/dock.

### /ɒ/ vs /ɔː/
cot/caught, pot/port, not/nought, spot/sport.

### /ʊ/ vs /uː/
full/fool, pull/pool, look/Luke, could/cooed.

### /ɑː/ vs /ʌ/ and /ɜː/
cart/cut, calm/come, heart/hurt, far/fur, stir/star.

### Diphthong contrasts
beer/bear (/ɪə/-/eə/), here/hair, go/saw (/əʊ/-/ɔː/), by/bay (/aɪ/-/eɪ/).

## Consonant contrasts

### /b/ vs /v/
berry/very, ban/van, best/vest, boat/vote, curb/curve, bowel/vowel.

### /s/ vs /z/
ice/eyes, price/prize, bus/buzz, face/phase, loose/lose, racer/razor.

### /ʃ/ vs /s/
she/see, ship/sip, sheet/seat, shave/save, sham/Sam.

### /ʃ/ vs /tʃ/
share/chair, sheep/cheap, shop/chop, cash/catch, shoes/choose.

### /θ/ vs /s/ and /t/
think/sink, thing/sing, mouth/mouse; thin/tin, three/tree, bath/bat.

### /ð/ vs /d/
they/day, then/den, there/dare, though/dough, breathe/breed.

### /w/ vs /v/
west/vest, wine/vine, wet/vet, worse/verse.

### /r/ vs /l/
right/light, road/load, rate/late, correct/collect.

### /ŋ/ vs /n/
sing/sin, thing/thin, ran/rang, sinner/singer, wing/win.

## From the original study notes

love/laugh, leave/live, virgin/version, trouble/travel, face/phase, beard/bird,
beer/bear, further/farther, very/bury.

## Practice

Use pairs as a daily drill: say both, exaggerate the contrast, then use each in
a short sentence. See [study links](study-links.md) for minimal-pair quizzes
(Marcus) and exercises (hot/hat, cheese/cheat).

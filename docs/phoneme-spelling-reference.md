# Phoneme-spelling reference (SSBE)

> An authoritative, exhaustive mapping of every British English phoneme to all
> the ways it can be spelled, including one-off spellings, silent letters, and
> the sound alternations that mark grammar. This page summarises the source and
> points to where its detail lives in the wiki.

Source: "The phonemes of British English and their spelling" (9 pages,
archived in `archive/`). It is organised Angle-2 style (sound -> spellings),
the exact complement of the wiki's per-sound pages. This page leads with the
**common** spellings for everyday use; rare and one-off spellings are kept in
a labelled [appendix](#appendix-rare-and-one-off-spellings) at the bottom.

## What the source covers

- Every vowel, diphthong, and consonant phoneme with its full spelling set.
- One-off / rare spellings per phoneme (e.g. /iː/ from "oe" in foetus, "ae" in
  Caesar, "is" in debris).
- Silent letters, by letter and rule (see [silent letters](silent-letters.md)).
- Grammar-marking sound alternations (below).
- A detailed yod (/j/) distribution (below).

## Unique additions folded into the wiki

### /θ/ - /ð/ alternation (noun vs verb)

Spelling signals it: nouns end -th (voiceless /θ/), verbs end -the (voiced
/ð/).

| Noun /θ/ | Verb /ð/ |
|---|---|
| bath | bathe |
| breath | breathe |
| sheath | sheathe |
| teeth | teethe |
| wreath | wreathe |

Also north/northern, south/southern, worth/worthy use /θ/ -> /ð/. Position
rules: initial th in function words is /ð/ (the, this, then), but content words
are /θ/ (think, through); medial th + -er/-ing is usually /ð/ (other, bathing)
with exceptions (panther, ether, anything). See
[/θ/](cons-th-voiceless.md) and [/ð/](cons-th-voiced.md).

### /s/ - /z/ alternation (noun vs verb)

Final -se can be /s/ or /z/; some pairs are spelled to mark it:

| Noun /s/ | Verb /z/ |
|---|---|
| advice | advise |
| device | devise |
| licence (UK noun) | license (UK verb) |
| practice (UK noun) | practise (UK verb) |

Invisible in spelling: abuse, close, diffuse, excuse, house, use (noun /s/,
verb /z/). Fixed: decrease, increase, promise, release always /s/; fuse,
surprise always /z/. See [/s/ vs /z/](cons-z.md).

### Yod (/j/) distribution

/j/ appears before /uː/ and /ʊə/ when spelled u, ue, eu, ew, ure, and NOT
preceded by j, ch, r, or consonant+l.

- With yod: tube, duty, music, fuel, due, cue, Tuesday, feud, neutral, ewe,
  few, new, mural, euro, pure, mature.
- Without yod (dropped): Jew, June, juice, chew, chute, rude, crew, prune,
  blue, plural, sluice.

The two-syllable /ɪə/ often compresses to /jə/ (audience, idiot, India); /uə/
to /wə/ (usual, innocuous). See [the letter U](letter-u.md) and
[long u /uː/](vowel-long-u.md).

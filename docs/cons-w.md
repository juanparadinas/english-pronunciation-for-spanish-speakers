# Consonant: /w/

> A rounded glide. Spanish has a similar sound, but the b/v/w confusion and the
> hard-g insertion (huevo -> "güevo") carry over as errors.

## 1. What it is

/w/ is a voiced labial-velar approximant (glide), the sound in "water", "we",
"quick".

Spanish contrast: Spanish has a /w/-like glide (hueso, cuando), so production
is possible. Errors: inserting a hard /g/ before it (like "güevo"), or mixing
it up with /b/ and /v/ (we/be/vee).

## 2. How to produce it

Round the lips tightly and raise the back of the tongue, then glide quickly
into the following vowel. No hard /g/ onset. Lips do not touch the teeth (that
would be /v/) and do not close (that would be /b/).

## 3. Angle 1 — when a spelling makes /w/

| Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|
| w | water, we, win, away, wonder, forward |
| wh | what, when, where, which, why (/w/ in most SSBE) |
| u (after q, g) | quick, queen, language, penguin, persuade |
| o (in "one/once") | one, once (/wʌn/) |

## 4. Angle 2 — where the /w/ sound is written

w: water, we, win, away, wonder, forward, well, work, want, world.
wh: what, when, where, which, why, while, white, wheel.
qu (= /kw/): quick, queen, question, quality, quiet, require.
gu (= /gw/): language, penguin, anguish.
u: persuade, suave.
o: one, once.

## 5. Minimal pairs / contrasts

| Pair | Contrast |
|---|---|
| west / vest | /w/ vs /v/ |
| wine / vine | /w/ vs /v/ |
| we / be | /w/ vs /b/ |
| wet / vet | /w/ vs /v/ |
| worse / verse | /w/ vs /v/ |

## 6. Common Spanish error

> The trap 1: inserting a hard g, so "water" gets a "gu" onset.
> The trap 2: confusing /w/, /v/, /b/ (west/vest, we/be).
> The fix: round the lips and glide, no g, no teeth, no lip closure. Contrast
> west/vest and we/be.

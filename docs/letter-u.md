# The letter U (two angles)

Like [the letter A](letter-a.md), the written U is highly predictable once you
read it both ways: given the spelling, which sound; given the sound, which
spelling.

## Angle 1 — how a written 'u' is pronounced

| Context | Sound | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closed syllable | /ʌ/ | cut, much, judge, luxury, under, sudden | ~99% of cases |
| Closed exceptions | /ʊ/ | put, push, bull, pull, bullet, full | learn these |
| Open syllable / Magic e | /juː/ or /uː/ | cube, cute, duke, tune, tube, presume, during | US often drops yod |
| u + R (stressed) | /ɜː/ long schwa | turn, purge, curve, further, turtle | always, barring exceptions |
| Unstressed | /ə/ or /ʊ/ | exposure, measure, culture | |

## Unwritten rule — yod drop after L or R

Open-syllable/magic-e U is /juː/, but the yod /j/ drops after L or R, even in
British English.

| Word | Expected | Actual | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| conclusion | /kluː.../ with yod | no yod | open syllable, L before |
| include | yod | no yod | L before |
| rude | yod | no yod | R before |
| blue | yod | no yod | L before |

Americans drop the yod far more widely (tune, duke, due, student, news).

### Precise yod distribution (from the source)

/j/ appears before /uː/ and /ʊə/ when the sound is spelled u, ue, eu, ew, ure,
and is NOT preceded by j, ch, r, or consonant+l:
- With yod: tube, duty, music, fuel, due, cue, Tuesday, feud, neutral, ewe,
  few, new, mural, euro, pure, mature.
- Without yod: Jew, June, juice, chew, chute, rude, crew, prune, blue, plural,
  sluice.

Optional yod (may be dropped, especially after θ, s, z, l): suit, sewage,
lute, absolute, revolution, salute, enthusiasm.

## Angle 2 — where /juː/ (and yod-drop /uː/) is written

Full yod candidate list (UK keeps most; US drops many; both drop after L/R):

actual, natural, virtual, eventual, perpetual, situation, mutual, mature,
youtube, tuesday, stupid, tune, tutorial, attitude, opportunity, gradual,
individual, procedure, education, schedule, module, during, duplex, duke, due,
deduce, reduce, produce, accurate, security, documentation, communicate,
formula, emulate, mutate, museum, argue, regulate, singular, annual, tenure,
nuke, manual, computer, pure, putrefaction, stipulate, tribune, albumen,
distribute, beauty, future.

## Angle 2 — where /ʌ/ (closed u) is written

cut, much, judge, luxury, under, sudden, public, publish, bubble, up, cut,
redundancy, concurrent, fun, sunday, drunk, pulse, assumption, summary,
sublimate, ugly, fluctuate.

Exceptions that take /ʊ/ instead: put, push, bull, pull, full, bullet.

## Common Spanish error

> The trap: pronouncing u+R as /ʌ/ (as if closed), so "turn" = "tun"; or
> keeping the yod where it drops (rude with a yod).
> The fix: stressed u+R is always long schwa /ɜː/. Drop the yod after L and R.

# Vowel: long u /uː/

> A long, tense, rounded, high-back vowel. Often preceded by a yod /j/, which
> Americans (and British after L/R) drop.

## 1. What it is

/uː/ is a long, tense, rounded high-back vowel, the sound in "food", "blue",
"tune". Often appears as /juː/ (with a yod) in spelling with u.

Spanish contrast: close to a long Spanish "u", so the vowel itself is easy. The
work is the yod (/juː/ vs /uː/) and keeping it distinct from short /ʊ/.

## 2. How to produce it

Tongue high and back, firm lip rounding, held long and tense. For /juː/, start
with a short y-glide (/j/) then the vowel.

## 3. Angle 1 — when a spelling makes /uː/ (or /juː/)

| Spelling | Sound | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| u (open / magic e) | /juː/ (UK), /uː/ (US) | cube, cute, tune, duke, use, music |
| u after L/R | /uː/ (yod drops) | rude, blue, June, conclusion, include |
| oo | /uː/ | food, moon, soon, pool, cool |
| ou | /uː/ | group, soup, route, through, youth |
| ew | /juː/ or /uː/ | few, new, flew, chew |
| ue | /uː/ or /juː/ | blue, true, due, argue |

## 4. Angle 2 — where the /uː/ sound is written

oo: food, moon, soon, pool, cool, room, boot, zoo.
ou: group, soup, route, through, youth, wound.
u / u-e: rude, June, flute, include, conclusion, super.
ew: few, new, flew, chew, crew.
ue: blue, true, due, argue, clue.
o (rare): do, who, move, prove, lose, tomb.

Yod-drop list (/juː/ -> /uː/ in US, and after L/R in UK): tune, duke, due,
during, reduce, produce, stupid, student, news, Tuesday, resume, consume,
suit, enthusiasm. Full list in [the letter U](letter-u.md).

Yod rule (from the source): /j/ before /uː/ appears with spellings u, ue, eu,
ew, ure unless preceded by j, ch, r, or consonant+l. So "new, few, cue" keep
the yod; "Jew, chew, crew, blue, prune" drop it.

## 5. Minimal pairs / contrasts

| Pair | Contrast |
|---|---|
| fool / full | /uː/ vs /ʊ/ |
| pool / pull | /uː/ vs /ʊ/ |
| Luke / look | /uː/ vs /ʊ/ |
| food / foot | /uː/ vs /ʊ/ |

## 6. Common Spanish error

> The trap: ignoring the yod, so "music" loses its /j/; or confusing /uː/
> with short /ʊ/ (fool/full).
> The fix: keep /uː/ long and tense; add the yod for u in open syllables
> (cube = /kjuːb/), but drop it after L and R (rude, blue).

# Vowel: long a /ɑː/ (back a)

> One of the top-four priority vowels for Spanish speakers, and the hard "a".
> A long, open, back vowel. In SSBE it reaches many more words than in American
> (the TRAP-BATH split). The front /æ/ is easy by comparison; this is the
> foreign one.

## 1. What it is

/ɑː/ is a long, open, back vowel, the sound in "car", "father", "past" (UK).
Open like Spanish "a" but further back and held long.

Spanish contrast: Spanish "a" is central and short. Spanish speakers make it
too short and too front, and often add a rolled R in "car".

## 2. How to produce it

Open the mouth wide, tongue low and back, lips neutral, held long. In British
English (non-rhotic), do not pronounce a following R; the long vowel carries
the sound.

## 3. Angle 1 — when a spelling makes /ɑː/

| Spelling | Examples |
|---|---|
| a + R | car, start, far, hard, park, large, March |
| a (UK TRAP-BATH) | past, path, class, last, fast, bath, dance, chance |
| al (silent l) | calm, palm, half, calf |
| a (foreign / misc) | father, drama, tomato (UK) |

## 4. Angle 2 — where the /ɑː/ sound is written

a + R: car, start, far, hard, park, large, March, garden, sharp.
a (UK only, TRAP-BATH): past, path, class, last, fast, bath, dance, chance,
grass, ask, after, example, command.
al (silent l): calm, palm, half, calf.
foreign: father, drama, banana (final), tomato (UK).

## 5. Minimal pairs / contrasts

| Pair | Contrast |
|---|---|
| cart / cut | /ɑː/ vs /ʌ/ |
| heart / hurt | /ɑː/ vs /ɜː/ |
| far / fur | /ɑː/ vs /ɜː/ |
| calm / come | /ɑː/ vs /ʌ/ |
| path (UK) / pat | /ɑː/ vs /æ/ |

## 6. Common Spanish error

> The trap: a short, front Spanish "a" plus a rolled R in "car".
> The fix: open, back, and long; no R in British English. For TRAP-BATH
> words, remember SSBE uses /ɑː/ where American uses /æ/.

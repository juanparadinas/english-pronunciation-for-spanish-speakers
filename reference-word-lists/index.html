    Reference word lists¶

    Curated word lists by phoneme, suffix, and pattern, plus minimal pairs and study video links. Source: personal study notes (ingles.txt).

    Magic e¶

    • a: fame, rate, blade, cake, mate, plate, page, pace, safe, same, sake, sale
    • e: pete, mete, these
    • i: bite, bike, cite, dive, dime, five, fire, file, fine, like, live, life, line, mine, mice, site, side, size, tide, tire
    • o: bone, coke, cone, doze, joke, lone, note, tone, vote
    • u: cube, cute, duke, dune, fuse, mute, muse, nude, tune, tube

    /z/¶

    use, please, expose, tuesday, close, was, lose, exam, exactly, exist, example, easy, is, as, realise, emphasize, size, advise, cause, pause, amuse, because, excuse, refuse, monetization, rationalization, specialization, automatization.

    /ʒ/¶

    pleasure, exposure, vision, genre, measure, closure, leisure, equation, massage; azure, presume, leisure.

    /ʃ/¶

    session, mission, machine, cache, she, musician, politician, pressure, assure, impression, discussion, expression, permission, progression, transmission.

    /j/ drop (yod)¶

    actual, natural, virtual, eventual, perpetual, situation, mutual, mature, youtube, tuesday, stupid, tune, tutorial, attitude, opportunity, gradual, individual, procedure, education, schedule, module, during, duke, due, reduce, produce, accurate, security, communicate, formula, museum, argue, regulate, annual, computer, pure, future.

    -ed verb endings¶

    • /ɪd/: accepted, admitted, appreciated, alerted, invited, presented, tested, wanted, tasted, trusted, requested; added, landed, provided, traded, included, depended, attended, avoided, decided, ended, extended, expanded.
    • /t/: attached, skipped, stopped, faced, fetched, flashed, introduced, jumped, laughed, missed, noticed, packed, reduced, talked, worked, washed.
    • /d/: admired, advised, annoyed, changed, merged, allowed, apologised, encouraged, labeled, lied, moved, named, pulled, refused, welcomed, bombed.

    Minimal pairs¶

    love/laugh, leave/live, virgin/version, trouble/travel, face/phase, beard/bird, beer/bear, further/farther, very/bury.

    Suffixes¶

    • -ician: musician, politician
    • -ize/-ise: emphasize, realize, size
    • -ence: evidence, reference, confidence, convenience, independence
    • -dule: schedule, module
    • -cade: cascade, facade, barricade, decade, arcade
    • -zation: monetization, rationalization, organization, automatization
    • -gine: engine, imagine
    • -ture: adventure, culture, structure, architecture
    • -sure: sure, ensure, leisure, disclosure, pleasure, exposure, measure
    • -ssion: impression, discussion, expression, permission, transmission
    • -sion: television, confusion, conclusion, supervision, decision, version
    • -sual: usual, visual, casual
    • -age: package, advantage, damage, garage, image
    • -nge: arrange, range, exchange, change
    • -sition: position, transition

    Long /ɜː/ spellings¶

    • ur: purpose, purge, precursor, disturb, burglary, burst, turbulence, turn
    • wor: world, work, worse, worth, worm, worry (US); not "worn"
    • er: permanent, jersey, server, permit, emerge, versus, observe, reverse, concern, personal; not "merely"
    • ir: confirm, bird, girl, virgin, circle, shirt, birth

    OU (unstable, ~11 pronunciations)¶

    • /ʌ/ enough, young, couple, touch, rough, encourage, country
    • /ɒ/ cough, trough
    • /ɔː/ course, court, four, source, board, bought, brought, ought, thought
    • /ʊ/ could, should, would
    • /uː/ group, soup, route, through
    • /ɜː/ journey, journal
    • /ə/ famous
    • /aʊ/ sound, house, loud, mount, out, announce, doubt, around, south, count
    • /əʊ/ although, shoulder
    • /ʊə/ tour

    Diphthongs¶

    • /eɪ/ day, stay, play, make, rate, great, eight, able
    • /aɪ/ try, life, like, mind, find, night, right, buy
    • /ɔɪ/ toy, boy, annoy, noise, join
    • /aʊ/ now, how, house, round
    • /əʊ/ home, go, so, low, slow, don't, road
    • /ɪə/ here, ear, cheer, peer, dear, deer, beer
    • /ʊə/ tour, cure, pure, mature, during, sure, poor
    • /eə/ there, where, care, stare, air, chair, hair, their, bear

    -ate suffix¶

    candidate, integrate, delegate, certificate, template, fluctuate, separate, climate, appropriate (nouns/adjectives → /ət/; verbs → /eɪt/).

    Noun/adjective vs verb stress shift¶

    progress, record, delegate, address, project, report, comment, request, command, contract, impact, abstract.
